Testing methodology

We tested the Wi-Fi Smart Lock for 30 days on two door types. Measured auto-unlock and battery life.

Real-world installation

Installation took 14 minutes. The main friction was aligning the retrofit plate on older deadbolts.

Ecosystem integration

Auto-unlock worked reliably. HomeKit was stable. Alexa required cloud linking.

Performance notes

Battery lasted 3.5 months. Auto-unlock triggered about 90% of the time.

Drawbacks

Auto-unlock not 100% reliable. Fit issues on non-standard deadbolts.

What owners say

Owners like the retrofit design. Some note auto-unlock misses and fit limitations.

Our conclusion

Best for renters who want smart locking without changing their deadbolt.
