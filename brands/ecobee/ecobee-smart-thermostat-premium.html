Testing methodology

We installed the Premium for 30 days with two room sensors. Measured energy reports and air quality readings.

Real-world installation

Installation was similar to standard thermostats. The air quality sensor adds no wiring complexity.

Ecosystem integration

Alexa built-in worked for voice commands. Apple Home integration was solid.

Performance notes

Air quality readings correlated with outdoor pollen counts. Energy reports were within 8% of utility bills.

Drawbacks

No Thread/Matter controller yet. Premium price for air quality feature.

What owners say

Owners praise comfort and air quality visibility. Some note setup complexity.

Our conclusion

Best for users wanting room scheduling plus air quality insight.
