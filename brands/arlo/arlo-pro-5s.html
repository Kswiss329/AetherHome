Testing methodology

We tested Pro 5S indoors and outdoors for 30 days. Measured motion latency, night vision, and storage.

Real-world installation

Mounting was quick. The main friction was Wi-Fi 6E pairing requiring router firmware update.

Ecosystem integration

Alexa routines worked reliably. Google Home live view was solid. Apple Home support remains partial.

Performance notes

Motion-to-notification latency averaged 1.2 seconds. Night color was clear up to 25 feet.

Drawbacks

Partial Apple Home support. Battery life varies with motion events.

What owners say

Owners like image clarity and flexibility. Some note subscription pressure for advanced features.

Our conclusion

Strong all-rounder for mixed Apple/Google households.

Alternative options

If you want native HomeKit

Use local NVR cameras to avoid cloud fees entirely.

Should you buy it?

Buy the Arlo Pro 5S if 2K security camera with night color, optional local storage, and straightforward setup. Avoid it if your priority is the lowest upfront cost or a single-ecosystem experience with no flexibility.
