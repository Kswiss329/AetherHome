Testing methodology

We tested Ultra 3 for 30 days. Evaluated 4K detail, AI detection, and SmartHub reliability.

Real-world installation

Setup required SmartHub. The main friction was hub placement for optimal signal.

Ecosystem integration

Alexa and Google Home worked well. SmartHub created a separate network for cameras.

Performance notes

4K detail was excellent. AI detection was accurate but required subscription for full features.

Drawbacks

Requires SmartHub for full functionality. Premium ongoing costs for AI features.

What owners say

Users praise clarity and field of view. Some note hub and subscription trade-offs.

Our conclusion

Best for users who prioritize top video quality and SmartHub integration.

Pros

  • 4K HDR video
  • 180-degree view
  • Advanced AI detection
  • Good low light

Cons

  • SmartHub required
  • Subscription for AI
  • High upfront cost

Alternative options

If you want 2K without hub

The Arlo Pro 5S offers excellent quality without SmartHub dependency.

If you want maximum compatibility

Consider cameras with native Thread/Matter support for future-proofing.

Should you buy it?

Buy the Arlo Ultra 3 if 4K HDR camera with 180-degree view and advanced AI detection. Avoid it if your priority is the lowest upfront cost or a single-ecosystem experience with no flexibility.
