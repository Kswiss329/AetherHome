Testing methodology

We used Echo Show 8 for 30 days for routines, video, and device control.

Real-world installation

Setup was simple. The main friction was camera permissions during initial setup.

Ecosystem integration

Alexa routines worked well. Zigbee devices paired without separate hub.

Performance notes

Display was bright enough for kitchen use. Audio was room-filling for a small device.

Drawbacks

Best for Alexa households. Camera privacy requires management.

What owners say

Owners find the display useful. Some note camera and Alexa limits.

Our conclusion

Best kitchen display for Alexa-centric homes.

Alternative options

Should you buy it?

Buy the Echo Show 8 if 8-inch smart display with spatial audio and built-in Zigbee hub. Avoid it if your priority is the lowest upfront cost or a single-ecosystem experience with no flexibility.
