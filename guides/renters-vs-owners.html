Renters vs owners

How to automate without drilling, and how to take your smart home with you.

No-drill options

Use retrofit locks, adhesive-mounted sensors, plug-in switches, and battery cameras. Avoid wiring changes and permanent mounts.

Portable kits

Pick a small hub and battery-powered devices. These move easily and preserve your deposit.

Owners

You can plan whole-home systems with wired sensors, centralized hubs, and multi-room audio. Start with infrastructure before adding devices.
