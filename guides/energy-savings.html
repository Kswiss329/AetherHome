Energy savings with smart devices

Practical ways to reduce bills without sacrificing comfort.

Smart thermostats

Schedule heating by room and time. Use occupancy sensors to avoid heating empty spaces. Our tests show savings of 8-15% compared to static schedules.

Smart lighting

Turn off unused lights automatically with motion sensors. Dim or shift schedules to match daylight patterns.

Leak and presence sensors

Prevent costly water damage and unnecessary heating. Early detection is cheaper than insurance deductibles.
