Automation recipes

Copy-friendly routines for common smart-home tasks.

Lighting

Turn on porch lights at sunset, dim living room lights after 10 PM, and flash hallway lights when a door opens at night.

Climate

Lower heat when windows open, raise temperature 30 minutes before wake-up, and hold energy-saving mode when nobody is home.

Security

Arm cameras when away, announce package arrivals, and send an alert if a leak sensor triggers.
