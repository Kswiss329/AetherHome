Smart home security and privacy

Protect your home data without giving up convenience.

Network segmentation

Place smart devices on a separate guest Wi-Fi network. This limits lateral movement if a device is compromised.

Camera placement and privacy

Avoid bedrooms and private spaces. Use physical covers when possible. Review cloud storage retention and download settings.

Firmware and access controls

Enable automatic updates where available. Use strong unique passwords, disable UPnP if you understand the tradeoffs, and turn on two-factor authentication.

Local-first when possible

Prefer devices with local control and optional local storage. This reduces exposure to cloud outages and unauthorized access.
